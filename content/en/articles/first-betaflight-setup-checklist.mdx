A new build's first Betaflight setup has a natural order — skipping ahead
usually means redoing an earlier step once something doesn't behave.

## 1. Firmware and ports

Flash the correct target firmware for your flight controller, then
configure ports (UART assignments for your receiver, VTX, and any other
peripherals) before touching anything else — nothing downstream works
without this being correct.

## 2. Receiver

Set the correct `serialrx_provider` matching your receiver's protocol, bind
it, and confirm channels move correctly in the Receiver tab.

## 3. Modes

Assign your arm switch and at least one flight mode (Angle is the standard
first choice) to a switch before your first power-up with props on.

## 4. Motor direction and mixer

With props off, confirm each motor spins the correct direction for your
mixer and prop rotation, and that motor order matches the configurator's
layout — this is safety-critical, not a tuning preference.

## 5. Failsafe

Configure and bench-test failsafe procedure with props off before ever
arming with props on. See <em>Radio Link &amp; Failsafe</em> in the
Knowledge Hub for what to actually test.

## 6. First flight defaults

Fly the first flight on close-to-default PID and filter values in Angle
mode, in an open area, and log it — the Tuning Guide's process starts from
that first blackbox log, not from default values alone.
