Plenty of pilots run into this:

- The quad shakes, even though the PID values look fine
- Motors run warm or hot easily
- Video feed glitches, breaks up, or shows tearing
- No matter how much you adjust PID, it never feels truly "locked in"

More often than not, these problems **aren't PID at all** — they're
something called **noise**.

## What is noise?

Noise is unwanted interference that gets mixed into the gyro data,
making the flight controller "believe the craft is shaking or moving"
when in reality the airframe may be perfectly still.

The result:

1. The FC keeps commanding motor corrections constantly
2. The quad shakes
3. Motors run hot
4. Flight feels unsettled

## Why is noise dangerous?

Because it causes:

- PID to work harder than it needs to
- Filters to have to work overtime
- Slower overall response
- A tune that never feels sharp or locked in

And worst of all, it tricks you into thinking "I need to adjust PID"
when that was never the actual root cause.

## The truth every pilot should know

> If noise is still high, no matter how good the PID values are —
> even from the best-tuned quad in the world — it will never truly feel locked in.

The correct order is to fix noise/filtering first, then move on to PID —
see <a href="/en/knowledge/gyro-dterm-filters">Understanding Gyro &amp;
D-term Filters</a> in the Knowledge Hub for more.

## EP1 summary

**Noise is the silent enemy of FPV drones.** It's the root of many other
problems, and needs to be understood before touching PID and filters.
